## What it does

`main.ts` (Node stdlib only, no network, fully deterministic) writes a single
line of plain text to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$ENTRY_OUTPUT` when set): the
excuse a gamer texts their boss to skip work on GTA 6 launch week.

The excuse is a mundane lockout with two concrete, escalating details — locked
out at 7am, a locksmith who cannot come until 10, and a spare key stranded
with family in another city. Boring, specific, and almost believable, with the
laugh coming from the excuse's own logic rather than a joke bolted on.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

The output is one line, 18 words, 100 characters — inside the gate of 1–140
characters and at most 20 words.
